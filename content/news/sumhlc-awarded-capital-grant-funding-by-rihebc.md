# SUMHLC Receives Capital Grant Award from RIHEBC!

![](https://sumhlc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/rihebc1-689x1024.jpg)

SUMHLC was pleased to welcome representatives from the Rhode Island Health and Educational Building Corporation (RIHEBC).This significant investment will support our ongoing efforts to expand access to high-quality behavioral health training, education, and workforce development opportunities across Rhode Island. We are grateful for RIHEBC’s partnership and commitment to strengthening the behavioral health workforce and improving outcomes for the individuals, families, and communities we serve.

Pictured: (left to right): John J. Tassoni, Jr., President/CEO, Dylan Zelazo of RIHEBC; and Sophia Elanani, Director of Training
