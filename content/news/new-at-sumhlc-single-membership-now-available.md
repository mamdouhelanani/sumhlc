Individual professionals can now join the SUMHLC community with our new **Single Membership** for **$300 per year**. Stay connected with SUMHLC communications, attend Board meetings as a non-voting participant, and showcase your affiliation with the **SUMHLC Proud Member** logo. It’s a great way for independent professionals and small private practices to be part of a network of behavioral health professionals and affiliates!
