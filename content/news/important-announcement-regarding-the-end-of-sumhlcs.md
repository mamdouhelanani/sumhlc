The Substance Use and Mental Health Leadership Council of Rhode Island (SUMHLC) would like to inform all participants that our contract with LearnUpon will be ending on April 4th, 2025.

Starting April 5th, 2025, we will solely be utilizing Zoom Webinars for all upcoming training sessions.

**ACTION REQUIRED:**

**We ask our participant community to please be sure to download your certificates from your LearnUpon dashboard by no later than April 3, 2025**

**After this date, access to the LearnUpon platform and certificates will no longer be available.**
