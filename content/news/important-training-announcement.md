IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT:

**We are sad to announce that we did not win the bid for the new BHDDH training contract. Our current contract ends on November 15,2024. Training will be held on a limited basis. OTP Health Home trainings will continue as scheduled. We want to thank everyone who has participated in our trainings throughout the years! Although we will be providing training on a limited basis, SUMHLC will continue our advocacy and collaboration work to promote a cooperative, coordinated system of high quality, comprehensive community-based mental health and substance use prevention and treatment services**.
